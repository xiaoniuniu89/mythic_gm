# Variations

Mythic GME 2nd Edition provides optional rules and mechanical variations to customize the emulation experience, support prepared modules, or track long-term campaigns.

---

## 📈 1. The Thread Progress Track
A visual tracking mechanism designed to keep the narrative focused on resolving a specific key plotline.
- **Track Length:** Choose a **10, 15, or 20-point track** depending on the desired duration of the quest.
- **Progress Points:** Award **2 points** when characters make forward progress toward resolving the focused thread during a scene.
- **Flashpoints:** Key climactic events. Award 2 additional points. A flashpoint can trigger automatically once a threshold is reached on the track, generating a Random Event scoped to the current context.
- **Discovery Check:** If stuck, ask a Fate Question: *"Is something discovered?"* (min 50/50 odds). On a Yes/Exceptional Yes, roll on the Discovery Check Table to trigger clue drops or new directions.
- **Plot Armor:** The focus thread **cannot be resolved** through normal play while the progress track is active. Out-of-bounds narrative results must be interpreted to keep the thread alive.
- **Conclusion:** Once the track is full, a final Random Event triggers to conclude and close the thread.

---

## 🔑 2. Keyed Scenes
Keyed Scenes allow players to prepare custom triggers that inject specific events when conditions are met.
- **Trigger:** Define a trigger condition (e.g. *Entering the sorcerer's tower* or *Sleeping in the woods*).
- **Event:** Define a specific occurrence that overrides the next scene's normal setup. When the trigger condition is met, the next scene begins immediately with the keyed event.

---

## 🗺️ 3. Using Prepared Adventures
Allows running pre-published modules using Mythic GME as the GM simulator:
- **Diminisher Value:** Define a fraction (e.g. 1/2, 1/3) to scale down challenges, encounters, and NPC stats to fit a solo/small party.
- **Expected Scene Testing:** When testing scenes, rolling $\le$ Chaos Factor does not alter or interrupt the scene. Instead, the scene plays as expected but **starts with a Random Event**.
- **Prepared Adventure Event Focus Table:** Replaces the standard Focus table with entries tailored to module progression (focusing on module encounters and keys).

---

## 💎 4. Peril Points
An expendable player resource to control narrative outcomes and avoid premature character deaths:
- **Default Pools:** Players start with **2 Peril Points**.
- **Usage:** Expend a Peril Point to force-avert any mechanical outcome that would end the campaign (such as a lethal attack or a failed escape check), rewriting the description into a narrow escape or partial setback.
